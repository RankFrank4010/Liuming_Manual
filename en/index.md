## What is LiuMing?

LiuMing is an exercise community founded in 2026, covering all grades from primary school to senior high school across **nine subjects**, and offering one-stop learning services including online practice, automatic grading, paper assembly, contests, team collaboration, and AI grading.

Or, put another way, you can:

- Find the problems you need in the question bank and practice. For fill-in-the-blank and multiple choice questions, the system returns the result automatically; for other types, you can designate someone you trust or grade them yourself.
- Take part in online contests to improve your skills.
- Communicate through tools such as forums and articles.
- Use the team features to upload private problems, assign homework, host private contests, and manage your team.
- Let the system automatically generate papers for practice.

### Feature Highlights

- **All nine subjects covered**: Math, Chinese, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English, Geography, History, and Politics, broken down by grade level and difficulty, with contest star ratings preserved.
- **Automatic grading**: objective questions such as fill-in-the-blank, multiple choice, and equivalent expressions are graded instantly; subjective questions can be self-graded or graded by someone you trust, and certain subjects also support AI grading.
- **A closed learning data loop**: a wrong answer notebook, knowledge point radar, knowledge graph, and weak point analysis make every practice session traceable.
- **Paper assembly and the full paper library**: enter problem IDs to generate a professionally typeset PDF paper in one click, or share and download complete paper files.
- **Team collaboration**: private problems, homework, contests, and data dashboards for classes and clubs.
- **Parental supervision**: parents can link a student account (with the student's consent) and keep track of their learning remotely.

For a full overview of the features, see [Feature Overview](/en/basic/features). For detailed instructions, look up the matching page under "Guides" in the left sidebar as needed.

## Quick Start

New to LiuMing? Follow the steps below and you can start practicing within a few minutes.

### ① Register and Log In

LiuMing supports **multiple ways to log in**: username + password, phone number + SMS, Passkey (passwordless via fingerprint / face), and OTP dynamic codes. Pick whichever you like. You only need a verified phone number to access LiuMing, with no extra registration required.

> See [Registration and Login](/en/basic/account) for the detailed process and common questions.

::: warning About mainland China phone numbers
**Accounts without a verified mainland China phone number will have limited functionality.** Please do not register with disposable email addresses or phone numbers; such accounts may not work properly or may be banned at any time.
:::

### ② Complete Your Profile

After logging in, we suggest starting with **Account Settings**:

- Maintain your **role and grade level** (used to build your learning profile and recommendations).
- Choose an **editor mode**: visual mode (what you see is what you get) or code mode (Markdown source). The choice applies uniformly to every input box across the site.

::: tip You can switch editors anytime
Both modes save Markdown, so switching never loses content. If you're not familiar with Markdown, starting with **visual mode** is the most hassle-free.
:::

### ③ Start Practicing

- **Practice directly**: open the [Question Bank](/en/basic/bank), filter by subject, grade level, question type, difficulty, and tags, then answer problems online.
- **Do the calculation specials**: in primary and junior high school and want to drill calculations? Go to the [Calculation Zone](/en/basic/calc) for batch problem generation and instant grading.
- **Follow problem lists**: learn systematically by topic in [Problem Lists](/en/basic/problem-list), or build your own.
- **Join contests**: sign up for open contests, or take part in team / private contests, to test your real level.

> For the detailed use of each feature, see [Feature Overview](/en/basic/features) and the matching pages under "Guides".

### ④ Join the Community

LiuMing is a **community-built** platform, and you're welcome to contribute:

- **Contribute problems**: anyone can contribute problems with formula editing and attachment uploads. They are published after review by admins, and you can earn points.
- **Create problem lists / upload full papers**: organize topic collections and share paper resources; these also count as community contributions.
- **Write columns / post discussions**: share study insights and problem solutions in the [Discussion Forum and Columns](/en/basic/features#discussion-and-columns).
- **QQ user group**: chat with more students in real time and get quick answers. Group number: **1098142902**.
- **Report violations**: if you find rule-breaking content, report it through the ticket system to help keep the community clean.

> Before contributing, please read the [Community Rules and Penalties](/en/policies/rule) and [Academic Standards](/en/academic/) to avoid being penalized for not knowing the rules.

## Code of Conduct

You should read the (User Agreement) and (Community Rules). If you don't follow the rules, you will face the corresponding penalties.

- Don't violate any law of the People's Republic of China or of your own country.
- As much as possible, don't discuss topics unrelated to contests. Sensitive content, controversial content, unauthorized advertisements, large amounts of meaningless content, and pranks are strictly prohibited.
- Don't use disposable email addresses or phone numbers. Accounts without a verified mainland China phone number will have limited functionality.
- Don't deliberately waste the site's resources (for example by crawling, reverse engineering, or wasting computing resources such as holding up grading queues).
- When you run into a problem, check the site's Q&A, the documentation, and the community. If the information in the first two could solve your problem but you still waste community resources, you will be penalized. Everything is described in detail in the feature documentation.

Take good care of your account. Appeals claiming that "someone else used my account maliciously" will not be accepted. As long as malicious behavior occurs on an account, both the account and the people involved will be penalized, no matter who did it. When using this site on a public computer, never use features like password autofill or "remember me", and log out before you leave.

### Academic Integrity

LiuMing is a platform for learning and practice; please cherish it:

- **Do your own work**: practice is meant to test yourself. Don't copy or answer for others just to pad your stats or points.
- **Use AI honestly**: when using AI to assist your learning, you must follow the [AI Usage Guidelines](/en/academic/ai). Directly copying AI output to submit or answer will be treated as a violation.
- **Respect problems and solutions**: when contributing or quoting others' content, mind the copyright and originality requirements in the [Problem and Problem List Guidelines](/en/academic/problem) and [Solution and Article Guidelines](/en/academic/article).

::: danger Stat padding and paid boosting
Deliberately inflating practice data, hiring others to take contests, or paying someone to do homework counts as a serious violation. Depending on the violation cycle and the repeat-offense mechanism, you may receive a warning, a mute, or even a ban.
:::

### Civil Communication

The community's discussion forum and columns are places for learning:

- Stick to the topic, respect differing opinions, and discuss problem solutions rather than attacking people.
- Search before asking to avoid repeated questions; when posting, clearly state the problem, its ID, and your own thought process.

### Reporting and Appeals

- **Report**: if you find rule-breaking problems, problem lists, or inappropriate remarks, submit a report through the ticket system (images can be attached). An admin will review and handle it.
- **Appeal**: if you disagree with a ban or a content review result, you can submit an appeal with your reasons, and an admin will review it. Every ban is fully recorded as a ticket and can be traced throughout.

> For the complete mechanism of reporting, bans, and appeals, see [Account and Security](/en/basic/account-safety).

## When You Run into Problems

Don't panic when something goes wrong. Follow the steps below and most problems can be solved quickly:

### Step 1: Check the Documentation

- First, search this **Help Center** by keyword for the matching page. Every feature has its own documentation, written in detail.
- Then check the **Q&A (Frequently Asked Questions)**; many repeated questions are collected there.

::: tip
**If the documentation or FAQ can solve your problem, please don't waste community resources.** Try to solve it yourself with the information in the first two first; only ask in the community if you still can't. Repeatedly asking about things already explained clearly in the documentation may lead to penalties.
:::

### Step 2: Go to the Community

- Ask in the **LiuMing discussion forum**, but remember to search first in case someone has already asked.
- When asking, include: the problem ID, your steps, what you observed (screenshots are better), and what you've already tried.

### Step 3: Use Tickets / Reports

- Problems that need the platform to handle, such as feature failures, account anomalies, or rule-breaking content, should be submitted through the **ticket system**. Results can be traced throughout.
- If you disagree with a penalty, submit an appeal through a **ban ticket** and attach your reasons.

### Common Questions at a Glance

- **Can't log in?** Check whether your phone number is verified; see [Registration and Login](/en/basic/account).
- **Doubts about an answer?** Objective questions are judged by the system; for subjective questions, you can contact the grader or submit a content appeal.
- **Insufficient quota for paper assembly / AI grading?** Quotas are issued weekly based on your plan; you can check usage in your personal center. See [Grading Center](/en/basic/grading).
- **Want to know why you were banned?** After logging in, you'll see a clear ban notice (including the reason) that guides you into the appeal process. See [Account and Security](/en/basic/account-safety).
