# Listening Audio Clip Editing Guide

When generating a listening paper, you can insert an **audio clip** into a listening **section** — trimming the needed range from a question's audio. **LiuMing listening** (system TTS) can be **inserted as the whole segment**; **uploaded MP3** audio can be **trimmed before insertion**.

This page covers **how to use it**; listening papers and TTS configuration are in [Listening TTS Details](/en/basic/listening).

## 1. Where to Use It

1. Go to the "Paper Generation" page and open the **listening editor**.
2. Under a **listening section**, click "**Audio clip**" (add audio clip).

## 2. Pick the Question Audio

1. A dialog "**Choose question audio**" opens.
2. Search by **question number or statement keyword**, and choose the audio to insert.
3. Each audio carries a source badge:
   - **LiuMing listening**: generated by system TTS, **inserted as the whole segment** (no trimming needed);
   - **Uploaded audio**: an uploaded MP3 that can be **trimmed before insertion**.
4. Click "**Insert whole segment**" to insert directly, or "**Insert trimmed**" to open the clip editor.

## 3. Trim the Audio (Clip Editor)

1. The "**Listening clip editor**" opens; wait for the **waveform** to parse (a failure shows "Audio load failed").
2. **Drag on the waveform** to select the range to keep; **drag the two handles** to fine-tune the start/end.
   - The top shows "Duration Ns" and "Selected s-e s"; with no selection it says "No selection (using the whole segment)".
   - Use "**Select all**" / "**Clear selection**" for quick adjustments.
3. Click "**Save selection**" to keep the range, or "**Insert trimmed**" to insert it into the listening.
4. An inserted clip shows its range "{s}s-{e}s" or "Whole segment"; click "**Adjust clip**" to edit it again.

## 4. Common Issues

- **Audio load / parse failed**: usually an audio format or size issue; try another audio or re-upload.
- **Inserting without selecting**: it counts as "whole segment", i.e. the full audio is used.
- **Does uploaded audio go in whole?** No — only "LiuMing listening" (system TTS) is inserted whole; uploaded MP3 must be trimmed yourself.
