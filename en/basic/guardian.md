# Parental Supervision Details

Parental supervision lets parents **link their child's account** and view the child's learning data and status from a "parent" perspective, with AI analysis providing targeted suggestions, so parents can rest easy while their child focuses on learning.

## Linking a Child (Supervision Relationship)

Supervision is **authorized by invitation** from the child to the parent, establishing a one-way "supervised person → supervisor" relationship:

- **Start the binding**: the child (student account) initiates a supervision invitation by entering the parent's **nickname + email** on the binding page.
- **Parent accepts**: the parent account can accept / decline the binding request in the "Supervision Requests" section.
- **Identity verification**: when the binding is established, the relationship to the child that the parent filled in (such as an email) is verified to confirm the supervision relationship is genuine and valid.
- **Unlink / manage**: the parent can unlink or change children, or the supervised student can remove the binding.

::: note The child's consent is required
A supervision relationship must be **initiated and authorized by the child**; parents cannot bind another person's account unilaterally. This protects the child's privacy and autonomy.
:::

## Viewing Learning Data

After linking, parents can switch between the dimension-based data of each **supervised child** in the "Parent Center":

- **Overall statistics**: total submissions, correct / wrong counts, and accuracy (percentage).
- **Today's data**: today's submissions / today's correct / accuracy.
- **By subject**: submissions, correct counts, and accuracy for each **subject**.
- **By knowledge point**: submissions, correct counts, and accuracy for each **knowledge-point tag**, pinpointing the specific knowledge points the child is strong or weak at.
- **By day / by subject / by tag**: switch views to see daily trends.

## AI Analysis

The parent side provides **AI learning analysis**:

- The system builds a prompt from the statistics most relevant to the child's learning (overall, today's, by subject, by knowledge point) and calls AI to output a **report analyzing the child's recent learning performance with targeted suggestions**.
- It helps parents quickly grasp: which subject is weak, which knowledge points need focused reinforcement, and how to improve.

> AI analysis is a supplementary reference; parents can still judge for themselves alongside the [learning data](/en/basic/practice-stats) (wrong-answer book, knowledge graph).

## Supervision Operations

- **Multiple children**: one parent can supervise several children at once, switching between their independent data.
- **View switching**: switch between "child · data view" modes in the Parent Center to see different dimensions.
