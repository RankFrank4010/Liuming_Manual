# Full Paper Library Details

The Full Paper Library is LiuMing's community for sharing complete exam papers: any user can **upload a complete paper file** (PDF / Word / PPT / TXT) to share with the community, and can also **browse and download** full papers uploaded by others. It's suitable for sharing midterm / final / mock papers, unit papers and other complete exam sets.

> The Full Paper Library (complete paper files) differs from the "[Paper Generation](/en/basic/paper)" feature: Paper Generation **generates paper PDFs online by question number**; the Full Paper Library, on the other hand, **uploads real complete paper files** for the community to share.

## Browsing and Downloading

After logging in, go to the "Full Paper Library" page to browse the full papers uploaded by the community:

- **All / My uploads**: switch between all community papers and only the ones you uploaded.
- **Filter by status**: pending review / approved / rejected.
- Each full paper shows: **subject, education stage, title, uploader, file type / size, upload time, status, download count**.
- Click "**Download**" to save the corresponding paper file to your local machine (login required).

## Uploading a Full Paper

Anyone (login required) can upload, and the process is as follows:

1. Click "**Upload full paper**".
2. Fill in:
   - **Paper title** (required).
   - **Subject**, **education stage** (for categorization and search).
   - **Description** (optional): notes about the paper, its source, applicable grades, etc.
   - **File** (required): supports **PDF / Word / PPT / TXT**, up to **50MB** per file.
3. Optionally "**Link a problem list**": associate one or more public problem lists with this paper, so browsers can discover its accompanying question practice.
4. After submitting, the status becomes **pending review**, waiting for an administrator to review it.

## Review and Status

Uploaded full papers need administrator approval before they become visible to the community:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending review** | Submitted, waiting for an administrator to handle it (visible only to you in "My uploads"). |
| **Approved** | Approved, visible to the community, downloadable, and appearing in the "All" list. |
| **Rejected** | Not approved, with a **reason for rejection** attached; you can re-upload a revised version if needed. |

Administrators handle papers in "Full paper review": **approve** / **reject** (reason optional) / **delete**.

## Reporting Violating Full Papers

If you find a full paper with issues such as "violating content, infringement, spam", you can file a **report** against that paper and fill in the report reason. The report goes into the [ticket system](/en/basic/account-safety#reports-and-the-ticket-system) for an administrator to review.
