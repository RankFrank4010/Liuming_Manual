# Privacy Policy

We value your privacy. This policy explains what information LiuMing collects when providing the Service, how we use and protect it, and when we share it with third parties.

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect information in the following scenarios:

- **Account and sign-in**: credential information needed for registration, sign-in, and identity verification (such as username, phone number, Passkey / OTP configuration).
- **Service usage data**: practice and submission records, paper assembly / grading usage, access logs, and device and browser information.
- **Third-party analytics**: third-party analytics services used to measure site usage (such as Google Analytics).

## 2. How We Use Information

The information collected is used only to:

- Provide and improve the Service;
- Maintain account and platform security and detect anomalies and risks;
- Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

## 3. Sharing of Information

We do not share your personal information with third parties except:

- With your explicit consent;
- When required by law or regulators;
- With partners necessary to deliver the Service (limited to the necessary scope).

## 4. Cookies and Analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, and measure usage. You can manage or clear cookies in your browser, though some features may be affected.

## 5. Minors

Minor users should read this policy together with their parents or guardians and confirm that guardian consent has been obtained before submitting personal information (see also the [Terms of Service](/en/policies/tos)). We process minors' information with the knowledge and consent of their guardians.

## 6. Your Rights

You can access, correct, or delete the personal information in your account; see [Account and Security](/en/basic/account-safety) for the relevant operations and limitations.

## 7. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as the Service and regulations change; updates will be posted on this page.
