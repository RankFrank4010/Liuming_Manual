# Discussion Board Guide

The LiuMing discussion board (https://liumingbbs.franj2.top) is LiuMing's official community for exchanging study tips, discussing problem solutions, sharing experiences, and asking for help. All LiuMing users can take part in discussions here and help create a friendly, professional learning atmosphere.

::: tip QQ User Group
In addition to the discussion board, we also have a **QQ user group**: **1098142902**. If you want to chat with more fellow students in real time and get quick answers, you're welcome to join!
:::

This page is for users new to the discussion board. It covers the basics of using the board, posting and replying rules, asking good questions, and frequently asked questions. Before taking part in discussions, please also read the [Community Rules and Penalties](/en/policies/rule).

## What Is the Discussion Board?

The discussion board is a standalone community site that pairs with the LiuMing main platform. Its main purposes include:

- **Asking for help**: when you come across a problem you can't solve or a concept you don't understand during practice, post a thread asking for help.
- **Exchanging solutions**: share multiple solutions to one problem, better approaches, or ask how others solved a problem.
- **Sharing experience**: share study methods, exam preparation plans, and notes on organizing your wrong answers.
- **Feedback and suggestions**: suggest platform features, or report issues you run into while using the platform.

::: tip How the discussion board relates to the main platform
The discussion board and the LiuMing main platform use the same account system: **log in with your LiuMing account and you can log into the discussion board**. No separate registration is needed. Your posts will be linked to your LiuMing account and public profile.
:::

## Basic Usage

### Browsing and Searching

- Open the discussion board homepage to browse the latest discussions; browse by category (section) to quickly find the areas that interest you.
- **Search before you ask**: someone has usually asked a similar question before. Use the search box at the top of the page and enter keywords (such as a problem number or concept) to check whether an answer already exists. This avoids duplicate questions and saves everyone's time.

### Logging In

- Log in to the discussion board with your LiuMing account (see [Registration and Login](/en/basic/account) for how to log in).
- You can browse without logging in, but actions such as **posting, replying, upvoting, and bookmarking** require you to log in first.

### Posting

1. Click the **New Topic** (or "Post") button on the page.
2. Pick the right **category** (section): different topics belong to different sections, and posting in the matching section makes it easier to get attention and replies.
3. Fill in the **title**: summarize what you want to ask or the topic in one sentence so others can understand it at a glance. Avoid uninformative titles like "Help" or "Come take a look."
4. Fill in the **body**: describe your question or the content you're sharing in detail (see [How to Ask Questions](#how-to-ask-questions) below for the requirements).
5. Publish once you've checked everything.

### Replying and Interacting

- **Reply** below a post to join the discussion; when replying to a specific comment, use the **quote** feature so the person knows you're responding to their point.
- Make good use of **@ mentions**: when you want a particular user to answer, mention them in your reply with `@username`.
- **Upvote** replies that you find helpful to show appreciation, and mark resolved questions as **Solved** so people who come later can reference them.

::: tip Posts can be edited
If you find a mistake after posting or want to add information, you can **edit** your own post from the post menu. You can also delete your own posts yourself if needed.
:::

## How to Ask Questions

Good questions are more likely to get high-quality help. When asking, try to provide complete information:

1. **State the problem clearly**: if you're asking about a specific problem, give its **number** (e.g. `M0001`) or its content. Don't just say "I can't do a problem."
2. **Explain what's wrong**: can't you solve it? Does your answer not match the expected one? Or is there a step you don't understand?
3. **Include your own attempts**: what methods have you tried, and where did you get stuck? This helps answerers explain things more precisely and stops others from redoing the whole problem for you.
4. **Add screenshots / formulas when necessary**: screenshots can illustrate a problem or error; use the editor's built-in formula feature to format formulas so they're easy to read.
5. **Choose the right section**: post your question in the section that matches the subject or topic.

::: tip Check yourself before asking
Many questions are already answered in the documentation. Please search the [Help Center](/en/) before asking. Repeatedly asking questions that the documentation already covers clearly may be seen as wasting community resources (see [Community Rules and Penalties](/en/policies/rule)).
:::

## Posting and Replying Rules

The discussion board is a public space. Please help maintain it together:

- **Stay on topic**: posts should be related to studying, problems, or the platform. Don't treat the discussion board as a place for idle chit-chat and flooding.
- **Use clear titles**: use titles that describe the content, and don't use titles that are attention-grabbing or misleading.
- **Be respectful**: discuss the problem, not the person. You may question a solution, but don't attack or insult others personally.
- **Don't post prohibited content**: don't post content that violates laws or regulations, politically sensitive material, pornography, discrimination, or unauthorized ads.
- **No spamming**: don't post the same content repeatedly, and don't flood the board with large numbers of meaningless posts / replies in a short time.
- **Academic integrity**: don't post solutions copied from others; cite the source when quoting others' content; when using AI-assisted writing, follow the [AI Usage Policy](/en/academic/ai).

::: danger Consequences of Violations
Violations of the above rules will be handled according to the [Community Rules and Penalties](/en/policies/rule). Minor cases may result in a warning or post deletion; serious cases may lead to a mute, a discussion board ban, or even an account ban.
:::

## Reporting and Feedback

- **Report prohibited content**: if you find a prohibited post or reply, use the **Report** feature below the post to submit it to the admins, who will review and handle it.
- **Report platform issues**: for platform feature or account issues, we recommend reporting through the main platform's **ticket system** first (see [Account and Security Details](/en/basic/account-safety)); it's handled more directly and is fully traceable.
- **Contact the admins**: in urgent situations or if you disagree with a decision, you can contact the admins through the discussion board to explain your situation.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: Do I need to register separately to log into the discussion board?

No. The discussion board and the LiuMing main platform use the same account system. Just log in with your LiuMing account (see [Registration and Login](/en/basic/account)).

### Q: Why can't I post / reply?

Usually this is because you're **not logged in** or your **account is restricted** (for example, you've been muted or banned from the discussion board). First make sure you're logged in; if your account is in a restricted state, you'll receive a clear notice when you log in, and you can appeal on that basis (see [Account and Security Details](/en/basic/account-safety)).

### Q: Do posts need to be reviewed?

Normally, posts are visible as soon as they're published. If an admin decides a post violates the rules, it will be deleted or hidden, and you may receive the corresponding handling.

### Q: Can I post anonymously?

No. Everything you post on the discussion board is linked to your LiuMing account, and you're responsible for your own content.

### Q: How can I find posts I've made before?

You can view all of your posts on your profile page or under "My Posts."

### Q: What should I do if nobody replies to my question?

- Check whether you **chose the right section**, your **title is clear**, and your **information is complete**.
- Try adding more details (such as the problem number, screenshots, and what you've tried) to make the question easier to answer.
- You can also keep working on it and post an update about your progress, or ask again in the related topic section.
