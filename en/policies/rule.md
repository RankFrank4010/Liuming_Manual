# Community Rules and Penalties
These rules are established to ensure the smooth operation of the community. They apply to every feature of this site.
These rules may be revised as circumstances change.

## Chapter 1 General Provisions

### Article 1 Purpose
These rules are established to maintain the orderly operation of the LiuMing community, ensure the fairness and stability of features such as the problem bank, practice, and contests, and provide every user with a safe, friendly, and honest learning environment. By using this site, users are deemed to agree to abide by these rules, and violations will be penalized accordingly under these rules.

### Article 2 Scope of Application
These rules apply to every feature of this site, including but not limited to: the problem bank and practice, judging and evaluation, paper assembly, the complete paper library, contests, teams, problem lists, the grading center, the discussion board, columns, private messages, the ticket system, points and entitlements, and so on. Any behavior carried out on this site, regardless of which feature area it occurs in, is governed by these rules.

### Article 3 Basic Principles
1. **Obey the law**: users must not violate the laws of the People's Republic of China or the laws of the user's location, and must not post illegal or non-compliant content.
2. **Be respectful**: respect others and communicate kindly. Do not post insults, discrimination, personal attacks, or similar content, and do not reveal others' privacy.
3. **Academic integrity**: answer truthfully, compete with integrity, do not plagiarize or cheat, and do not disrupt the evaluation and contest order.
4. While complying with these rules, users must also comply with the *Terms of Service* and all specialized policies; where a specialized policy has provisions, the specialized policy prevails.

## Chapter 2 Penalties

### Article 4 Types of Penalties
Users who violate the rules will receive one of the following penalties depending on the specific type and severity of the violation:
1. Warning: we will warn the user through various channels (such as email, in-site pop-ups, and so on).
2. Mute: the user is barred from posting in any area of this site.
3. Forum ban: access to the forum, or to specific forum features, is revoked for a limited period or indefinitely.
4. Account ban: logging into this site is prohibited for a limited period or indefinitely; a ban may come with additional restrictions (such as restricting practice, paper assembly, contests, and so on).
5. Other necessary measures.

::: tip
Every ban is fully recorded as a **ticket**, including how it was initiated and processed, the specific restrictions and duration, and the appeal and review results. The entire process is traceable. See [Account and Security Details](/en/basic/account-safety).
:::

### Article 5 Joint Liability
1. If a user violates the rules using one of their multiple accounts, or registers or uses other accounts after being penalized in order to bypass the penalty, all associated accounts will receive an additional penalty of equal or greater severity.
2. If there are frequent violations from the same source, the platform will impose access restrictions on that source; if you are certain that the restriction is caused by uncontrollable factors such as NAT, you can contact us to explain the situation and apply for an adjustment.
3. If a team commits serious violations, the team will be banned, and the founder and admins will be subject to joint liability. Users within the team who violate the rules will also receive corresponding penalties.

### Article 6 Enforcement of Penalties
1. **Reporting channels**: if you discover prohibited content or behavior, you can report it through the following channels:
   - Discussion board: use the report feature on prohibited posts and replies.
   - Ticket system: submit reports on prohibited problems, problem lists, complete papers, and so on, with the reason attached (images can be attached); the system will create a ticket and processing events at the same time.
2. **Admin handling**: for violations confirmed through verification, admins may **delete, hide, remove, or reject** the relevant content as appropriate, and impose the corresponding penalty on the violator in accordance with these rules.
3. **Public disclosure**: every ban is recorded as a ticket, and current and historical violation periods are publicly queryable; the public profile of a banned user clearly displays the ban status, and the user also receives a clear ban notice when accessing restricted features.

## Chapter 3 Violations and Penalty Details

### Article 7 Violation Period

::: danger Don't abuse the rules
The violation period and repeat-offender determination are designed to prevent users from being long-term discredited because of occasional past violations. If we are confident that you are treating the rules as a task checklist, we will, depending on the circumstances, gift you a ten-year violation period and a ten-year repeat-offender determination gift package.
:::

The 365 days starting from a user's first violation constitute one violation period. If the user violates the rules again within the violation period, the end of that violation period is extended to 365 days after the most recent violation. While the violation period is active, all violations within that period are displayed on the user's homepage.
**A "repeat offense" means violating, within the violation period, the same rule that was already violated in that violation period.**
After the violation period ends, its related effects will stop (including but not limited to the public disclosure of violations and repeat-offender determination), but the violations are still kept on internal record.
<br/>

**If you are penalized with a mute, account ban, or the like, the violation period will receive an equivalent punitive extension.**

When necessary, following the proper procedure, a specific extension of the violation period can be imposed on its own, along with the worst-case repeat-offender determination (that is, you are always treated as a repeat offender within the period).

### Article 8 General Disorderly Conduct

Users who engage in the following behavior in public areas (such as the forum) will receive a **warning** or **mute**. Serious cases and repeat offenders will face harsher penalties or an **account ban**:
1. Posting offensive, attention-grabbing content or pranks: first offense, a warning. Repeat offense, a one-week mute. Habitual offender, a one-month mute, increasing by one month each time.
2. Sending meaningless content on a large scale: first offense, a warning. Repeat offense, a six-month mute. Habitual offender, a six-month ban, increasing by six months each time.
3. Posting unfriendly content (insults, discrimination, personal attacks, and so on): first offense, a warning. Repeat offense, a two-week mute. Habitual offender, a six-month mute, increasing by one year each time.
4. Revealing others' privacy: first offense, a two-week mute. Habitual offender, a six-month account ban, increasing by two months each time.
5. Posting content in the wrong place: first offense, a warning; repeat offense, a one-week mute, increasing by one week each time.
6. Posting unauthorized ads: first offense, a six-month mute; habitual offender, a permanent mute.

### Article 9 Serious Disorderly Conduct
The following behavior will result in an **account ban**:
1. Posting content that violates the relevant laws of the People's Republic of China, sensitive topics (political content, self-harm, suicide, gambling, pornography, drugs, and so on), or remarks that seriously violate social morality: permanent ban.
2. Deliberately wasting this site's resources (such as scraping, reverse engineering, network attacks, or wasting computing resources (for example, blocking up the judge)): permanent ban.
3. Wasting this site's administrative resources, such as contributing meaningless problems: the relevant permissions will be revoked.
4. Creating multiple alt accounts on many occasions and using them to disrupt order: first offense, a one-year ban on the main account and a permanent ban on all alt accounts; repeat offense, a permanent ban on all accounts.
5. Other behavior that adversely affects the operation of this site: determined specially on a case-by-case basis.

### Article 10 General Violations of Academic Integrity

Behavior that violates academic integrity requirements in practice, evaluation, assignments, contests, and other scenarios will receive a **warning** or **mute** depending on the circumstances; in serious cases, the user will face an **account ban** or the **revocation of related contributions**. This includes but is not limited to:
1. Copying others' code or answers in evaluation or assignments, or directly submitting AI-generated code or answers while falsely claiming them as your own work (for the boundaries of AI use, see the [AI Usage Policy](/en/academic/ai)): first offense, a warning and revocation of that attempt's score; repeat offense, a two-week mute and revocation of the related scores; habitual offender, an account ban starting at one month, increasing each time.
2. Violating contest participation rules (such as one person participating with multiple accounts, playing on someone else's behalf, or sharing answers during a contest): handled under Article 12, and the related contest results will be canceled.
3. Contributing problems or problem lists copied from others, or plagiarizing others' work in complete papers, problem solutions, or column articles: first offense, revocation of the related contributions and a warning; repeat offense, a one-month mute and revocation of the related permissions; habitual offender, an account ban and permanent revocation of the related contributions.

### Article 11 Seriously Undermining Academic and Evaluation Order
The following behavior will result in an **account ban**; in serious cases, a **permanent ban**:
1. Mass-submitting meaningless answers (such as random, duplicate, blank, or clearly irrelevant answers) to interfere with evaluation: first offense, a warning and revocation of the related scores; repeat offense, a one-month account ban; habitual offender, an account ban starting at one year, increasing each time.
2. Maliciously gaming the judge (using scripts or exploits to batch-submit or repeatedly submit in order to manipulate scores or leaderboards, and so on): an account ban, with the duration depending on the circumstances.
3. Creating problems, problem lists, or complete papers that induce others to cheat (such as content designed for the purpose of cheating): permanent revocation of the related contributions and an account ban.
4. Repeated plagiarism without correction: those who are penalized multiple times for academic integrity issues within a violation period and continue to plagiarize will be treated as habitual offenders, subject to the maximum penalty under Article 10 and harsher treatment.

### Article 12 Contest-Related Violations
Participants must follow the [Contest Rules](/en/academic/competition) during contests. The following behavior will receive penalties such as **cancellation of results**, **mute**, or **account ban**; in serious cases, a **permanent ban**:
1. One person participating with multiple accounts: first offense, cancellation of the related results and a warning; repeat offense, a one-month account ban; habitual offender, a one-year account ban and a permanent ban on alt accounts.
2. Playing on someone else's behalf (competing for others or having others compete for you): first offense, cancellation of results and a two-week mute; repeat offense, a six-month account ban.
3. Sharing answers during a contest (asking for or leaking answers to others while the contest is in progress): first offense, cancellation of results and a warning; repeat offense, a two-week mute; habitual offender, an account ban starting at one month, increasing each time.
4. Using prohibited aids (such as attacking the system or cheating by exploiting vulnerabilities): a permanent account ban.

## Chapter 4 Reporting and Appeals

### Article 13 Reporting
1. **Reporting channels**: you can report prohibited posts on the LiuMing discussion board, or submit reports on prohibited problems, problem lists, and complete papers through the ticket system.
2. **Required materials**: when reporting, please explain the reason for the violation and attach necessary materials such as screenshots and links; reports involving problems and problem lists support attaching images to facilitate verification by the admins.
3. **Processing flow**: a report will create a ticket at the same time, and every status change generates an event (operator, reason, time), forming a traceable processing timeline.
4. **Malicious reports**: baseless or abusive use of the report feature to interfere with others or waste administrative resources will be handled under Article 8, "General Disorderly Conduct."

### Article 14 Appeals
1. **Ban appeals**: banned users can submit an appeal on their ban ticket with reasons attached. The admins may approve (lift the ban), reject (uphold the ban), adjust the penalty (change the ruling), or request supplementary materials. Those asked for supplementary materials should provide them on time; otherwise, the appeal may not be processed further.
2. **Content appeals**: for rejected problem or problem list contributions, you can submit an appeal to request a review, and the admins will give a final result after handling it.
3. **Appeal deadline**: both ban appeals and content appeals should be filed promptly after receiving the result; the specific deadline is subject to the ticket notice. Appeals filed late without good reason may not be accepted.

### Article 15 Leniency for Voluntary Confession
If users proactively explain the situation to the admins, admit their mistake, and cooperate with the handling before the violation is processed, the admin team may **impose a lighter or reduced penalty** depending on the circumstances. Voluntary confession does not affect the correction of the damage already caused.

## Chapter 5 Removal of Penalties

### Article 16 Removal of Penalties
1. **Automatic removal upon expiration**: penalties with a duration (mute, forum ban, account ban, and so on) are automatically removed after the period ends, and the ticket status is updated to "Closed."
2. **Requesting removal**: if you disagree with a penalty or believe it is no longer necessary, you can submit an appeal through the ban ticket, and the admins will decide whether to remove, adjust, or uphold it after evaluation.
3. **Evaluation mechanism**: the admin team will evaluate comprehensively based on factors such as the nature of the violation, the record of repeat offenses within the period, and corrective behavior; permanent penalties are not subject to automatic removal upon expiration and can only be decided at the admins' discretion through an appeal.

## Chapter 6 Supplementary Provisions

### Article 17 Definitions of Quantity
The quantities referred to in these rules are defined as follows:

| Expression | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| or more / or less | inclusive of the stated number |
| multiple times | two times or more |
| a large amount | more than multiple times, i.e., three times or more |
| first offense / repeat offense / habitual offender | per the determination rules of Article 7, "Violation Period" |

### Article 18 Effect of the Rules
1. Users must simultaneously comply with the *Terms of Service*, these rules, and all specialized policies (such as the [Academic Policy](/en/academic/) and contest rules).
2. Where a specialized policy is inconsistent with these rules, the specialized policy prevails; where a specialized policy does not cover a matter, these rules apply.

### Article 19 Special Discretion
Matters not covered by these rules, or individual cases with complex circumstances that are not suitable for direct application of the above provisions, will be decided by the admin team in the exercise of its authority. The admin team may take necessary measures not listed in these rules for specific circumstances (see Article 4, "Other Necessary Measures").

### Article 20 Effective Date and Revisions
1. These rules take effect from the date of publication.
2. These rules will be updated as appropriate as the platform develops; revised versions will be published on this page. Revisions do not have retroactive effect, except for continuing violations within a violation period.
